## The joke

> Monday mornings are proof my bed has separation anxiety — every week it begs me to stay, and every week I betray it for a meeting that could have been an email.

160 characters, under the 200-character cap. The twist is the betrayal:
the bed is the loyal one, the meeting is the homewrecker — and the meeting
didn't even need to exist.

## Run

```sh
node --experimental-strip-types main.ts
```

Writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt`.
